# MILESTONE 21 — ADVANCED RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE & AUTONOMOUS RESEARCH PLANNING

## PROJECT CONTEXT

I am building an AI Research Assistant.

Milestones 1–20 have already been completed.

The system currently supports the complete research lifecycle:

1. Authentication and Authorization
2. Research Workspaces
3. PDF Upload
4. PDF Text Extraction
5. Document Chunking
6. Embedding Generation
7. Vector Database
8. RAG Question Answering
9. LangGraph Autonomous Research Agent
10. Citation and Source Grounding
11. Advanced Paper Summarization
12. Multi-Paper Comparison
13. Literature Review
14. Research Gap Detection
15. Research Questions and Hypotheses
16. Research Proposal and Methodology Planning
17. Experiment Planning and Statistical Planning
18. Experiment Execution, Tracking and Results Analysis
19. Automated Research Paper and Final Report Generation
20. Productionization, Observability, Evaluation and Deployment

The system is now production-ready.

Milestone 21 must make the AI Research Assistant capable of performing higher-level research planning and research intelligence.

The goal is NOT to create an uncontrolled autonomous agent.

The goal is to create a:

    HUMAN-CONTROLLED
    EVIDENCE-GROUNDED
    TRACEABLE
    MULTI-STEP
    RESEARCH PLANNING SYSTEM

The system should be capable of taking a broad research objective and converting it into an actionable research plan.
