# Milestone 24 — Advanced Research Assistant UI & Research Intelligence Dashboard

## PROJECT CONTEXT

I am building an **AI Research Assistant** application.

The application allows researchers/students to:

* Upload research papers
* Process PDF documents
* Extract text
* Chunk documents
* Generate embeddings
* Store vectors
* Perform RAG-based question answering
* Use an autonomous LangGraph research agent
* Generate grounded answers
* Display citations and sources
* Summarize research papers
* Compare multiple research papers
* Maintain research conversations
* Explore research information through a frontend application

The backend and core frontend workflow have already been implemented.

---
