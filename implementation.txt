# 11. NODE 3 — RESEARCH PROBLEM DEFINITION

Generate a concise research problem statement.

The problem statement should explain:

1. Current state of the research area
2. What is missing
3. Why the missing area matters
4. What the proposed research intends to investigate

Example:

```text
Existing RAG systems frequently rely on static knowledge bases.
However, limited research evaluates how continuously updated
knowledge affects retrieval and answer quality. This study proposes
to evaluate the impact of knowledge freshness on RAG performance.
```

The statement must be grounded in the literature.

---

# 12. NODE 4 — RESEARCH MOTIVATION

Generate a research motivation section.

Explain:

* why the problem matters
* practical importance
* scientific importance
* limitations of existing approaches
* why the proposed study is worth investigating

Do not exaggerate importance.

Do not claim:

> "This is the first study..."

unless the evidence genuinely supports such a claim.

Prefer:

> "The reviewed literature indicates limited evaluation of..."

# 13. NODE 5 — RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

Reuse Milestone 16 objectives.

Validate that they are aligned with:

* research question
* hypothesis
* methodology

If necessary, refine them.

Generate:

### Primary Objective

One central objective.

### Secondary Objectives

2–5 supporting objectives.

Example:

```text
Primary Objective:
Evaluate whether hybrid retrieval improves RAG retrieval performance.

Secondary Objectives:
1. Compare dense and hybrid retrieval.
2. Evaluate retrieval performance using Recall@K.
3. Measure downstream answer factuality.
4. Analyze performance across query categories.
```

# 33. NODE 16 — EXPECTED OUTCOMES

IMPORTANT:

These are NOT experimental results.

Use language such as:

> "The study is expected to determine whether..."

Do not generate fake numerical results.

Bad:

> Hybrid retrieval will improve Recall@10 by 15%.

Better:

> Hybrid retrieval is expected to improve retrieval performance if lexical and semantic signals provide complementary evidence.
